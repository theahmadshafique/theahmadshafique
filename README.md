# Muhammad Ahmad

Karachi-based web developer. I turn ideas into live, secure Laravel platforms that real businesses run on every day: online stores, learning platforms, school management and AI sales automation.

## What I have built

### ALIF TAWAKKAL - [aliftawakkal.com](https://aliftawakkal.com)

A complete e-commerce platform for a pure honey, desi ghee, dates and dry fruits brand. It covers the storefront with product variants, batch-tracked stock, cart and checkout, bank-transfer payments with payment proof, order tracking, courier and delivery handling, returns, coupons and automated notifications. Behind it sit a staff admin panel protected by mandatory two-step verification and a partner portal for resellers.

### Live Quran Reading - [livequranreading.com](https://livequranreading.com)

A live platform where children learn the Quran online with verified teachers. It started as a single academy and grew into a multi-tenant platform: many academies, such as Sarsawi Quran Academy, run on the same system, each with its own branding, pricing, students and teachers. It includes live video classes, scheduling, attendance, progress tracking, billing, regional payment gateways, WhatsApp and SMS reminders, public lectures, an interactive whiteboard, a blog and automated backups.

### Islamic Info - [islamic-info.com](https://islamic-info.com)

An Islamic knowledge website built to make Islamic information easy to find and pleasant to read.

### Classifolio - [classifolio.com](https://classifolio.com)

A school and academy management system for schools, academies and coaching centers. It brings student records, classes, fee collection with PDF receipts, attendance, WhatsApp automation for parents, a parent portal, tests and marks, report cards, ID cards and a teacher module into one place.

### NexOrbit AI Sales Agent Engine - in development

An AI sales agent platform that talks to customers and helps businesses turn conversations into orders. Launching soon on its own domain.
